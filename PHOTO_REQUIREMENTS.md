## 🎨 **PHOTO STYLE GUIDELINES**

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Color Palette**: Warm earth tones (terracotta #B8735F, sage green #8B9A7A, warm beige #EAE8E4, dark brown #2A2624)
- **Lighting**: Natural, soft, directional (avoid harsh shadows)
- **Composition**: Asymmetric, generous negative space, editorial quality
- **Mood**: Refined luxury, accessible sophistication, movement & grace

### Technical Requirements
- **Resolution**: Minimum 2x display size for retina screens
- **Format**: WebP preferred for web, with JPG/PNG fallbacks
- **Color Space**: sRGB for web display
- **File Size**: Optimized for web (under 500KB for most images, under 2MB for hero images)

### Photography Style
- **Product Shots**: Clean, professional, white/neutral backgrounds
- **Lifestyle Shots**: Aspirational but authentic, real studios/homes
- **Detail Shots**: Extreme close-ups showing material quality
- **Environmental**: Natural settings, warm lighting, lived-in spaces
