# Research: Pilates Reformer for Digital Nomads in Mexico

## Topic Overview
**Title:** Pilates Reformer for Digital Nomads in Mexico
**Target Audience:** Digital nomads, expats, and remote workers living in Mexico (CDMX, Tulum, Playa del Carmen, etc.)
**Goal:** Guide them to find studios, understand pricing, and maintain their fitness routine while traveling.

3.  **Community:** Meeting other expats/nomads.
4.  **Locations:** Top studios in nomad hubs (Roma/Condesa in CDMX, Tulum).
5.  **Cost:** Price comparison with US/Europe.
